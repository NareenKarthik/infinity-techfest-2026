General Hackathon Rules

The following 10 rules apply to all rapid prototyping participants. Failure to adhere may result in point deductions or disqualification.

1

Prototype Delivery Within Allotted Time

Teams must submit a working prototype within the allotted time.

2

Permitted AI and Tool Usage

Participants may use AI coding assistants, open-source libraries, and permitted APIs.

3

Transparency on Implemented vs Simulated

Teams must clearly explain which features are implemented and which are simulated.

4

Core Functionality Demonstration

The prototype must demonstrate the core functionality of the selected problem statement.

5

No Faked Live Detections

Participants must not present sample or simulated results as genuine live detections.

6

Attribution and Citation

All datasets, APIs, and external resources used must be acknowledged.

7

Legal, Privacy and Consent Compliance

Teams must respect privacy, consent, copyright, and applicable laws.

8

Restricted Sensitive Data

Participants must not upload private or sensitive recordings without proper permission.

9

Uncertainty & Responsible Claims

Detection systems must communicate uncertainty and must not make unsupported claims.

10

Live Demonstration & Evaluation

Judges may evaluate the prototype through a live demonstration and questions.
